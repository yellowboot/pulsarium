How to read the four gravity modes

🌌 Sector Galaxies

Each of the 19 SEC industries forms a rotating galaxy. Its glow and rotation speed reflect 12-month revenue growth and operating margin shifts across comparable companies.

🕳️ SEC Risk Black Hole

Pulls in companies showing early-warning balance-sheet or cash-flow deterioration in 10-K/10-Q filings: rising net income with falling operating cash flow, negative TTM cash flow with rising debt, bloated inventory/receivables, or uncovered dividend payouts.

🌟 Supernova Catalysts

Highlights companies with forward-looking positive inflection points: operating profit or cash-flow turnarounds, Form 4 insider buying clusters, active share count reductions via buybacks, and P/E discounts to their industry.

🛰️ Portfolio & Basket X-Ray

Separates a tracked basket of stocks into three diagnostic zones at a glance—Supernova Catalysts, Steady Orbit, and SEC Risk Black Hole warnings. In the personal cabinet, this runs automatically on your own portfolios and watchlists.

Zero random links

When you select a company, connecting filaments show its direct peers in the same SEC industry so you can compare sales growth, industry P/E, and filing health side by side.

Direct EDGAR verification

Every risk warning and turnaround catalyst links directly to the underlying SEC Form 10-K, 10-Q, or Form 4 filing on sec.gov.
