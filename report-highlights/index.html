How this is calculated

Exchange-listed operating companies from the sector universe, with comparable USD US-GAAP data and at least $100 million of TTM revenue. All matching turnarounds are available in the details; share changes and stress include up to 24 companies each. Each metric has its own coverage. Full histories expand from shared screens and remain cached. Share changes and stress are ranked by TTM revenue.

Operating losses turning into profit come first, ranked by the reported quarterly profit swing in USD: current profit minus prior-year loss. Operating margin change breaks equal-dollar ties, then TTM revenue and ticker. Current and prior quarterly profit margins use their respective quarterly revenues. Operating income must change from at most -$1 million to at least +$1 million YoY, with margins crossing -0.5% and +0.5%. Two-quarter persistence requires three contiguous fiscal quarters. Cash-flow turnarounds follow, ranked by the quarterly operating cash-flow swing in USD. The latest YoY comparison must cross from negative to positive; both current quarters must be positive and improve YoY by at least $1 million and 10% of the prior amount. Banking and insurance are excluded from the cash-flow screen. Cumulative cash-flow statements are converted to standalone quarters by exact period boundaries.

At least 2% YoY change in weighted-average basic common shares for comparable fiscal quarters, using both figures from the same SEC filing. Published comparative figures incorporate stock and reverse-stock splits. Basic shares avoid diluted-EPS denominator changes when a company moves between loss and profit. Buybacks labels require both fewer average shares and reported repurchase cash; other reductions are labelled Share reduction. Dilution labels mean a higher average share count, which can reflect compensation, option exercise, acquisitions or financing. Selected acquisition explanations are separately sourced, dated and expire as the comparison moves beyond the event. Buyback cash retains its exact period and can coexist with net share growth.

Non-financial businesses require negative operating cash flow over four contiguous fiscal quarters plus cash reserves down at least 20% and $10 million, or reported current plus noncurrent debt up at least 25% and $25 million. Balance-sheet comparisons use the same filing and disclose both dates. Banking and insurance are excluded from this screen. Missing components are excluded rather than set to zero. These are report-based warning signals, not default probabilities.

Figures come from 10-Q and 10-K reports on SEC EDGAR and describe reported results, not prices. This is not investment advice.

Questions

What is a profit turnaround?

A company whose quarterly operating income went from a loss a year earlier to a profit now, as reported to the SEC. Cash-flow turnarounds, where operating cash flow turned positive, follow them.

What do buybacks and dilution mean here?

Buybacks: fewer average shares than a year earlier, with cash spent on repurchases in the same report. Dilution: more shares, which can come from stock compensation, acquisitions or new financing.

Is financial stress a prediction?

No. It flags report-based warning signs, such as twelve months of negative operating cash flow with cash down at least 20% or debt up at least 25%. It is not a default probability or investment advice.
