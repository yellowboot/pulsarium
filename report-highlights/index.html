How this is calculated

Exchange-listed operating companies from the sector universe, with comparable USD US-GAAP data and at least $100 million of TTM revenue. All matching turnarounds and profit/cash, payout and working-capital highlights are available in the details; share changes and stress include up to 24 companies each. Coverage is reported separately for each screen; full-history checks cover a subset of the universe. Full histories expand from shared screens and a bounded large-company queue, and remain cached. Share changes and stress are ranked by TTM revenue; new checks rank by relative divergence.

Operating losses turning into profit come first, ranked by the reported quarterly profit swing in USD: current profit minus prior-year loss. Operating margin change breaks equal-dollar ties, then TTM revenue and ticker. Current and prior quarterly profit margins use their respective quarterly revenues. Operating income must change from at most -$1 million to at least +$1 million YoY, with margins crossing -0.5% and +0.5%. Two-quarter persistence requires three contiguous fiscal quarters. Cash-flow turnarounds follow, ranked by the quarterly operating cash-flow swing in USD. The latest YoY comparison must cross from negative to positive; both current quarters must be positive and improve YoY by at least $1 million and 10% of the prior amount. Banking and insurance are excluded from the cash-flow screen. Cumulative cash-flow statements are converted to standalone quarters by exact period boundaries.

At least 2% YoY change in weighted-average basic common shares for comparable fiscal quarters, using both figures from the same SEC filing. Published comparative figures incorporate stock and reverse-stock splits. Basic shares avoid diluted-EPS denominator changes when a company moves between loss and profit. Buybacks labels require both fewer average shares and reported repurchase cash; other reductions are labelled Share reduction. Dilution labels mean a higher average share count, which can reflect compensation, option exercise, acquisitions or financing. Selected acquisition explanations are separately sourced, dated and expire as the comparison moves beyond the event. Buyback cash retains its exact period and can coexist with net share growth.

Non-financial businesses require negative operating cash flow over four contiguous fiscal quarters plus cash reserves down at least 20% and $10 million, or reported current plus noncurrent debt up at least 25% and $25 million. Balance-sheet comparisons use the same filing and disclose both dates. Banking and insurance are excluded from this screen. Missing components are excluded rather than set to zero. These are report-based warning signals, not default probabilities.

Non-financial companies. Compare reported net income and operating cash flow for the latest reported fiscal year-to-date or annual period of at least 170 days. Both YoY comparisons must have the same period boundaries and accession. Use ProfitLoss where disclosed, otherwise NetIncomeLoss; retain the same profit concept across both periods. Positive net income must rise at least 10% and $10 million while operating cash flow falls at least 20% and $10 million, or positive net income of at least $10 million accompanies negative operating cash flow of at least $10 million. Ranked by the net-income minus cash-flow gap as a share of TTM revenue. The figures describe a divergence; they do not establish its cause or an accounting problem.

Non-financial companies. Use the latest reported year-to-date or annual cash-flow period of at least 170 days. Free cash flow here is reported total operating cash flow minus cash payments for property, plant and equipment. All components must have exactly matching periods and accession. Reported common-share buybacks and dividends are shown separately; an absent component is excluded from the reported subtotal, never treated as zero. The reported payout subtotal must exceed positive free cash flow by at least 20%, with a gap of at least $10 million and 1% of TTM revenue; negative free cash flow also qualifies. Ranked by payout gap / TTM revenue. This does not identify the funding source or predict a dividend cut.

Non-financial companies. Inventory or net current receivables must grow at least 20% YoY and at least $10 million, outpace comparable quarterly revenue growth by at least 15 percentage points, and increase by at least 1% of TTM revenue. Revenue comparatives use the same latest filing and comparable fiscal-quarter boundaries. Balance-sheet comparatives use the latest available disclosure for the matching prior-year date (within seven days of the revenue comparison), with a separate SEC source when necessary. Both balance dates are shown. Ranked by the growth-rate gap versus revenue, then the amount / TTM revenue. Changes can reflect seasonality, acquisitions and business mix; they do not establish slower collection or unsold goods.

Buybacks: fewer average shares than a year earlier, with cash spent on repurchases in the same report. Dilution: more shares, which can come from stock compensation, acquisitions or new financing.

Is financial stress a prediction?
