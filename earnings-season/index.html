What the season showed

52% rose, 47% fell

Of 730 companies worth $10 billion or more, 381 closed higher on the first trading day after their report and 346 closed lower.

A beat was almost a coin flip

571 of 728 companies beat profit forecasts. 310 of them rose and 258 fell anyway. Of the 133 that missed, 60 rose.

The six giants all fell

Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta and Tesla each closed lower the day after reporting. Of 52 companies worth $200 billion or more, 40% rose.

Tech and energy took it hardest

57% of technology companies and 64% of energy companies fell. Utilities did best: 62% rose. Sectors as labelled by Nasdaq.

One in seven moved 10% or more

The biggest one-day moves: JFrog +35.3%, Glaukos +33.6%, AppLovin +31.0%; Reddit −29.2%, Intel −28.6%, ServiceNow −26.9%.

The market over the same weeks

The S&P 500 slipped 2.4% between October 12 and November 11, 2026. Nvidia reports on November 17.
