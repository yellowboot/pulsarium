Broker import · Trading 212

Import your Trading 212 trades into a portfolio tracker

Export your transaction history from Trading 212 and upload the file to Pulsarium's free cabinet: every trade is checked with you before it is saved, and your broker login never leaves your hands.

Free during the public beta · No broker login needed · Two-factor sign-in

1. Export the file from Trading 212

  1. Open HistoryIn Trading 212 open the menu, go to History and tap the export icon at the top right.
  2. Export CSVPick the period (up to one year per file), include Orders and Dividends, and tap Export CSV. For a longer history, export one file per year and upload them one after another.

2. Import it into Pulsarium

  1. Upload the fileIn the cabinet open Account → Export / Import CSV → Import trades from a broker and choose the file. CSV and TSV work; save an Excel file as CSV first.
  2. Check the columnsPick the target portfolio. Pulsarium pre-selects the date, operation, instrument, quantity and price columns — check them and set whether dates are day-first.
  3. Review every rowClick Review rows. Each trade is matched to a listing (the exchange is named, the most likely one is marked best match), already imported rows are skipped and possible duplicates are flagged. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

Questions

My Trading 212 history is longer than a year — what then?

Trading 212 exports at most one year per file. Export each year separately and upload the files one by one; overlapping rows are recognised and skipped.

Do I have to connect my broker account?

No. Pulsarium never asks for broker logins. You export a transaction file from your broker and upload it, or add trades by hand. Nothing reaches your broker account.

Is Pulsarium free?

Yes. The personal cabinet is free during the public beta: portfolios, dividends, watchlists, price alerts, broker import and the news feed. Paid plans with an AI research assistant are planned; the free features stay available.
