Broker import · Webull

Import your Webull trades into a portfolio tracker

Export your transaction history from Webull and upload the file to Pulsarium's free cabinet: every trade is checked with you before it is saved, and your broker login never leaves your hands.

Free during the public beta · No broker login needed · Two-factor sign-in

1. Export the file from Webull

  1. Export your ordersIn the Webull app open Account, pick the account, go to History and tap the export icon at the top right. In the desktop app it is the Orders widget's menu → Export Orders.
  2. Save the file from your emailConfirm your email address: within about 5–10 minutes Webull sends the CSV (Webull_Orders_Records.csv) as an attachment. Save it and upload it.

2. Import it into Pulsarium

  1. Upload the fileIn the cabinet open Account → Export / Import CSV → Import from a broker and choose the file. CSV and TSV work; save an Excel file as CSV first.
  2. Check the columnsPick the target portfolio. Pulsarium suggests the broker from the file name and pre-selects the date, operation, instrument, quantity, price and fee columns — check them and whether dates are day- or month-first. After a successful import the column choices for that file format are remembered on your device.
  3. Review every rowClick Review rows. Each trade is matched to a listing (the exchange is named, the most likely one is marked best match), already imported rows are skipped and possible duplicates are flagged. Nothing is saved until you confirm.

What Pulsarium does with a Webull file

Only filled orders

Cancelled and failed orders are skipped on their own; a partly filled order is imported for the shares that were filled, at their average price.

The US trading day

Webull writes order times in New York time (EST/EDT); every trade keeps the date Webull shows, after-hours trades included.

Questions

Which Webull file do I need?

The orders export Webull emails to you (Webull_Orders_Records.csv). Upload it as it is: it lists every order, and only the filled ones become trades.

Are Webull dividends imported?

Webull's orders file has no dividends. Pulsarium detects the dividends of the stocks you hold and shows them for your confirmation; once confirmed, they are recorded against the position.

Do I have to connect my broker account?

No. Pulsarium never asks for broker logins. You export a transaction file from your broker and upload it, or add trades by hand. Nothing reaches your broker account.

Is Pulsarium free?

Yes. The personal cabinet is free during the public beta: portfolios, dividends, watchlists, price alerts, broker import and the news feed. Paid plans with an AI research assistant are planned; the free features stay available.
